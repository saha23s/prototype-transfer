AudioProtoPNet-5-XCL on BirdSet HSN

Published numbers, our reproduction, and first unsupervised adaptation baselines on the new site/recordist splits. Last updated 2026-11-27. Source: docs/BIRDSET.md and docs/BASELINES.md on branch split/5-adaptation-baselines.

1. Setup

Model. Published AudioProtoPNet-5-BirdSet-XCL checkpoint (pinned revision), 21 HSN species, scored with BirdSet's metrics in fp32 (bf16 shifts cmAP by about 0.001).

Splits. Leakage-aware: focal recordings split by recordist, soundscapes split by site (train / val / test). For unsupervised adaptation the 100 soundscape sites are split into 3 partitions (seeds 0 to 2), each with 75 adaptation sites (9,000 clips, audio only, labels unused) and 25 test sites (3,000 clips, used only for scoring). Split files are fingerprinted in configs/splits/hsn.json; adaptation manifests containing test clips are refused.

Metrics. cmAP (class-wise mean average precision), AUROC, and top-1 accuracy (T1) counted over clips with at least one label.

4. Adaptation baselines (preliminary: untuned hyperparameters)

Inductive, source-free: start from the published checkpoint, adapt on a partition's 75 adaptation sites, score its 25 test sites.

  • No adaptation: the source model as is (Table 2).
  • Tent: minimise the mean binary entropy of the sigmoid outputs (multi-label Tent).
  • Pseudo-labels: BCE against hard pseudo-labels from the source model (p > 0.5), fixed before adapting.
  • Oracle: BCE against the adaptation clips' true labels. Upper bound, not a UDA method.
  • LayerNorm: only the backbone's LayerNorm affine parameters (40,192) are adapted; full: all 87.7M parameters. The ConvNeXt backbone has no BatchNorm, so AdaBN does not apply.

Tent and pseudo-labelling collapse (only 0.35% of pseudo-labels are positive; both objectives appear to push every species towards absent). These learning rates are not tuned: before any comparison, each method is to be tuned on BirdSet POW (never on HSN test sites), NOTELA added, and runs repeated over seeds.
