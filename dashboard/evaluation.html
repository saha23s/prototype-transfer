Evaluation protocol

One fixed way to score every model. Changing any part means re-scoring all models.

1 · Species

Secondary: at least 5 positives in both the focal and soundscape evaluation sets (12 species on test). Primary: also at least 30 soundscape positives (7 on test).

2 · Headline metric

Soundscape macro average precision over the primary species. Also reported: soundscape ROC-AUC, cmAP over the secondary species, and focal AUC / AP to check that focal performance is retained. Focal recordings are part of the model's pretraining data (Xeno-Canto), so focal scores are an in-domain reference.

3 · Prototype transfer

For each prototype, Δ = AUCsoundscape − AUCfocal of its maximum activation as a detector of its species. Fails if Δ < -0.10; improves if Δ > +0.02. 5 prototypes per species. Soundscape negatives are all other clips; as a sensitivity check they are also restricted to clips with another target bird, which matches the focal negatives.

4 · Uncertainty

Cluster bootstrap with 1,000 resamples shared across models, so differences are paired. Soundscape clips are resampled by site (30 test sites), focal clips by recordist (110). Intervals are 95% percentile intervals.

5 · Development

Method choices are made on the validation split or 3-fold site-grouped cross-validation. The test sites are kept for final numbers.

6 · Use of labels and audio

Soundscape labels are used to evaluate. Methods that adapt may use unlabeled audio of training sites, never audio of held-out sites. All models are scored on identical clips.

Success criteria for a method

All four must hold for a method to count as closing the gap.

Reading cross-validation numbers

  • Each fold has about 23 held-out sites. Results are shown per fold; a “mean ± sd” describes the spread over 3 folds and is not a confidence interval.
  • “Reliably better” means the fold's paired 95% interval of the difference to the pretrained model lies above zero (and “reliably worse”, below).
  • The scored species differ between folds, so failing prototypes are reported as a share of the prototypes scored on each fold, and method changes are also reported on the same species every fold (those primary on all folds, and those eligible on all folds).
  • The protocol's intervals compare each model with the pretrained model. The intervals between two adapted models on the results page use the same site resampling but are exploratory additions. Neither includes variation between training seeds.
  • Many methods are compared on the same folds without correction for multiple comparisons.
