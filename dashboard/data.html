What this page shows

  • Different class balance. The three most frequent soundscape species account for 72% of all soundscape species labels. Species that are common in focal data can be rare in the field.
  • Different clip content. Each focal clip carries one label. 55% of soundscape clips have no target species and 7% have two or more.
  • Species are clustered by site. Many species occur at only a few sites, so neighbouring clips are far from independent. This decides how the data must be split and how uncertainty must be measured.
