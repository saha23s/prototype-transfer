Problem formulation

Adapt an interpretable bird-sound classifier from focal recordings to soundscapes without soundscape labels.

Start with a bird-sound model trained on focal recordings. Using unlabeled soundscape audio from some recording sites, adapt it. Then freeze it and measure how well it recognises birds on soundscapes from other sites.

Model

A backbone maps the spectrogram x of a 5 s clip to patch embeddings zt,f(x). Species s has K = 5 prototypes psk. Each prototype's activation is its best match anywhere in the clip, and the species score is a non-negative weighted sum:

ask(x) = maxt,f ReLU( cos( zt,f(x), psk ) )
ŷs(x) = σ( Σk wsk ask(x) + bs ),   wsk ≥ 0

An error can therefore come from the activations ask (a prototype no longer matches, or matches the wrong sound), from how the weights wsk combine them, or from both.

Formal problem and objective

Given the pretrained model h0; optionally the labeled focal training clips DF; and soundscape sites split into training sites T and held-out sites H with T ∩ H = ∅. UT is the audio of T and YT its labels.

Target setting (inductive, no soundscape labels): find a procedure that returns

h = Adapt( h0, UT [, DF] )

and freeze h before it sees any audio from H. Methods that adapt on the audio being scored are outside this problem.

Score on H: soundscape average precision, averaged over the primary species PH (what the metric means):

MH(h) = (1 / |PH|) Σs∈PH APs(h; H)

Reference: h★ is the pretrained model fine-tuned on UT with YT. The share of the gap closed is

γH(h) = ( MH(h) − MH(h0) ) / ( MH(h★) − MH(h0) )

Goal: γ ≥ 50% with the gain reliably above zero, while keeping focal AUC within 1 point (out of 100) of h0. The full list is under success criteria. h★ is a reference from one training recipe, not a proven upper bound: a method or a single species can exceed it.

In development, H is one fold and T the other two; we report means over the folds, and γ as the mean gain divided by the mean gain of h★. In the final test, T is the original training sites and H the test sites.

Prototype transfer is measured by Δsk = AUCsoundscape(ask) − AUCfocal(ask), a diagnostic of where the model breaks, not the objective.

Research questions

  1. How much supervision does the gap cost? How many labeled soundscape clips are needed to close half of it? → Label curve
  2. Can it be closed without labels? By adapting activations or weights with unlabeled audio, or by making focal training look more like soundscapes? → Initial results
  3. Is prototype transfer the lever? When prototypes detect better on soundscapes, does average precision follow? (Observational comparisons across methods cannot show cause; that needs an intervention on prototypes alone.) → Initial results
