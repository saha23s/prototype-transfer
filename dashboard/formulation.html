Prototype transfer: focal → soundscape bird recognition

Problem formulation

Adapt an interpretable bird-sound classifier from focal recordings to soundscapes, ideally without soundscape labels.

Model

A backbone maps the spectrogram x of a 5 s clip to patch embeddings zt,f(x). Species s has K = 5 prototypes psk. Each prototype's activation is its best match anywhere in the clip, and the species score is a non-negative weighted sum:

ask(x) = maxt,f ReLU( cos( zt,f(x), psk ) )
ŷs(x) = σ( Σk wsk ask(x) + bs ),   wsk ≥ 0

An error can therefore come from the activations ask (a prototype no longer matches, or matches the wrong sound), from how the weights wsk combine them, or from both.

Setting

The source domain F (focal) has one labeled species per clip. The target domain S (soundscapes) is multi-label, includes many clips with no target species, and is grouped by recording site. Sites are divided into training sites and held-out sites. The task is multi-label recognition over 21 species on held-out soundscape sites.

Objective

Let M(h) be the soundscape macro average precision of model h over the primary species P, on held-out sites:

M(h) = (1 / |P|) Σs∈P APs(h)

With h0 the pretrained model and h★ the model trained on all labeled training-site soundscapes, the share of the gap closed by an adapted model h is

γ(h) = ( M(h) − M(h0) ) / ( M(h★) − M(h0) )

We look for an adaptation that uses no soundscape labels and reaches γ ≥ 0.5 while keeping focal AUC within 0.01 of h0 (full list under success criteria). h★ is an empirical reference, not a proven upper bound: a method or a single species can exceed it.

Prototype transfer is measured by Δsk = AUCS(ask) − AUCF(ask), a diagnostic of where the model breaks.

Research questions

  1. How much supervision does the gap cost? How many labeled soundscape clips are needed to close half of it? → Label curve
  2. Can it be closed without labels? By adapting activations or weights with unlabeled audio, or by making focal training look more like soundscapes? → Initial results
  3. Is prototype transfer the lever? When prototypes detect better on soundscapes, does average precision follow? (Observational comparisons across methods cannot show cause; that needs an intervention on prototypes alone.) → Initial results
