How prototypes transfer: focal → soundscape

Data splits

Every model is trained and scored on the same pinned splits. Focal clips are split by recordist and soundscape clips by recording site, because clips of one recording are near-duplicates and would leak between splits. The test split is used only for final numbers. Lighter to darker = train, validation, test.

Evaluation protocol (v1)

One fixed way to score every model; changing any part means a new protocol version and re-scoring all models. Details: docs/PROTOCOL.md. Code: src/prototype_transfer/evaluation/canonical.py.

1 · Species

Secondary: at least 5 positives in both the focal and soundscape test sets (12 species). Primary: at least 30 soundscape test positives (7: whcspa, amepip, rocwre, daejun, clanut, herthr, amerob).

2 · Headline metric

Soundscape macro AP over the primary species. Also reported: soundscape AUC, cmAP over the secondary species, and focal AUC / AP to check that focal performance is retained.

3 · Prototype transfer

For each prototype, Δ = AUCsoundscape − AUCfocal of its maximum activation as a detector of its species. Fails if Δ < -0.10; improves if Δ > +0.02. 5 prototypes per species.

4 · Uncertainty

Cluster bootstrap, 1000 resamples shared across models: soundscape clips by site (30 test sites), focal clips by recordist (110). Intervals are 95% percentile intervals; differences between models are paired.

5 · Development

Choices are made on the validation split or 3-fold site-grouped cross-validation (--split val, --split cv --fold F); the test sites are scored once, for final numbers.

6 · Honesty rules

Soundscape labels are used to evaluate and, for selection, only through val or the folds. Adaptation may use training-site audio without labels, never held-out audio. Every model is scored on identical clips (checked).

Success criteria for a method

All four must hold, measured against the baseline and the soundscape-only oracle.

#CriterionDefinition
1AP gap closedat least 50% of the gap between the baseline and the supervised soundscape-only model, and the 95% interval of the improvement over the baseline excludes 0
2AUC not below baselinesoundscape AUC (primary) is at least the baseline's
3Failing prototypes halvedat most half as many prototypes fail as for the baseline
4Focal retainedfocal AUC (primary) within 0.01 of the baseline's
