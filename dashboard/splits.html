Data splits

Splits are built so that no recording, recordist or site leaks into evaluation.

5,460focal clips from 655 recordists
12,000soundscape clips from 100 sites
60 / 10 / 30soundscape sites: train / validation / test
3site-grouped cross-validation folds over the non-test sites

Splitting rules

  • Focal clips are split by recordist. One person's recordings share equipment and place.
  • Soundscape clips are split by recording site. Neighbouring 5 s clips are near-duplicates, and species cluster by site.
  • Test sites are reserved for final numbers. Methods are compared by cross-validation over the other sites.
  • Splits are fixed. Every model is trained and scored on the same ones.
